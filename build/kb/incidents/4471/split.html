parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every product reads dates through one shared parser, and it reads month first. All of our tenants write the day first, so any date where both numbers are 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: 2 December becomes 12 February. This comes in through the app, by email, and through booking-v2 forms. It is what #4471 says it is.

hint: booking-v3 prints the wrong format under its date field

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The result looks the same as a parser flip, but the bug is in the form. The parser fix leaves it as it is. Only booking-v3 tenants get it, and only through their booking form. The desk’s own table on #4471 already marks these as not this ticket.

Intake tags do not help here. They are guessed from the reporter’s browser, so an en-US browser gets tagged hint wherever its tenant is. Go by tenant and channel (triage).

one-day: the date moves by one day

These reports are not a month flip. The day moves by one: Tuesday shows as Monday; a client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto get different days for the same meeting; an invite arrives before the booking was made; bookings in one month are one day off. That points at time zones when the invite is written, not at how a date is read. The desk noted them as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). The #4471 table counts them under parser by tenant, so the parser fix would leave them open.
